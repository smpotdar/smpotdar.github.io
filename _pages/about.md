I work as a Machine Vision R&D Engineer at Koh Young Research Canada(KYRC) in Vancouver, Canada, which is the research arm of <a href="https://kohyoung.com/en/" target="_blank">Koh Young Technology(KYT)</a> which has its HQ in Seoul, Korea. KYT makes machine vision techologies for providing optical inspection and measurement solutions for chip and PCB manufacturing factories. 

I enjoy working for this company as it allows me to develop vision algorithms which get deployed across the world at several factories where the tolerance for failure is very low. These algorithm are expected to be very fast and precise at the same time. 


#### Research Interest:
Broadly, I am interested in solving hard problems in Computer Vision and Robotics using tools from various fields like Machine Learning, Statistics and Geometry. Spe, I like to work on unsupervised and supervised long term tracking of objects, object detection and recognition. I also like to work on SLAM based projects and camera calibration.

#### Previously:
I was a Master's student in Carnegie Mellon University's (CMU) Electrical and Computer Engineering Department. I was also research intern in Prof. Katerina Fragidiaki's in CMU's Machine Learning Deparment where I worked on unsupervised object detection and tracking. During my master's, I also had a chance to work in Prof. Mario Saviddes's lab on fine grain object classification.
