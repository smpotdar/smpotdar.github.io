I work as a Machine Vision R&D Engineer at Koh Young Research Canada(KYRC) in Vancouver, Canada, which is the research arm of <a href="https://kohyoung.com/en/" target="_blank">Koh Young Technology(KYT)</a>. 

KYT makes machines which utilize machine vision technologies and optomechatronics for providing optical inspection and measurement solutions for semiconductor and chip manufacturing factories. These machines utililize a mixture of classical and deep learning based CV and ML techniques to inspect millions of components, and flag defective ones, every day across the world and are an essential part of the modern semiconductor and chip manufacturing pipelines. It is very likely that some components in the device that you are using to read this website were once inspected by a KYT machine (and possibly by an algorithm developed by our team :smile:)

As a R&D Engineer, my task is to develop machine vision algorithm which will be utilized by KYT machines world wide. This involves first developing a protype from scratch in Python, then developing production code in C++ and finally deploying on KYT machines through CI/CD. For more specifics information regarding my projects at KYT please read my <a href="https://smpotdar.github.io/assets/pdf/shubhankar_potdar_CV.pdf" target="_blank">resume</a>.

#### Academic Research Interest:
Broadly, I am interested in solving hard problems in Computer Vision and Robotics using tools from various fields like Machine Learning, Statistics and Geometry.
